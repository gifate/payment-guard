# Privacy Policy — Payment Guard

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Payment Guard is a Forge app for Jira and Jira Service Management, built for the Codegeist 2026 hackathon.

## What the app accesses
- The app reads work items (for example invoice and payment requests) on the Atlassian site where it is installed, only to check them and to show the result inside Jira.
- The app uses only the permissions listed on the Atlassian installation screen.

## Where data is kept
- The app runs on Atlassian's Forge platform. Any data it saves (for example a hold record and the reason a person gave for releasing it) is kept in Forge storage on Atlassian's infrastructure, tied to your site.
- The app does not send data to any server outside Atlassian.
- The developer does not receive, copy or sell your data. The developer does not store personal data in their own systems.

## Logs
- Atlassian may keep app logs as described in Atlassian's own policies. Site admins can turn log sharing on or off in Atlassian Administration.

## Removing data
- Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage for your site, as handled by Atlassian.
